abc-sim Finetune Losses

Training loss of three VLAs finetuned on the same 9 abc-sim episodes (25,155 frames at 30 fps), each on one H100 at batch 56 for about 2,000 steps. Each loss is flow-matching MSE over the 14 real joint dims.

Comparability. All three losses are velocity-target MSE in each model's own normalized action space. GR00T and DM0.5 average over the 14 real dims with padding masked out. π0.5 averages over 32 padded dims, so its curve here is loss × 32/14, an upper bound on its 14-dim MSE. The normalizations differ (GR00T min-max, π0.5 mean/std, DM0.5 quantile), so treat the curves as shapes, not exact numbers to rank.

Setups. GR00T N1.7: full finetune of the action head and projector with the VLM frozen, 40-step chunks, LR 1e-4 with 5% warmup and cosine decay to zero at step 2,000 (33 min on the H100). π0.5: LoRA, 50-step chunks, peak LR 2.5e-5, stopped at step 2,000 with the LR still near peak. DM0.5: LoRA, 50-step chunks, LR 1e-4 with 1k warmup, also stopped near peak LR. The training losses are smoothed per logging step: each point is the mean over the previous 10 steps.
